Privacy Policy

This site collects only what's needed to respond to enquiries sent through the contact form, email, or WhatsApp, such as your name, email address, and the details of your message.

Information you submit is used solely to reply to you about your project or enquiry. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you leave a comment or reaction on a project page, that comment is stored as a public GitHub Discussion and is visible to anyone viewing this site, since it's powered by GitHub's own comments system (Giscus). Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in comments.

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is used only to send you occasional updates about new work and design tips. You can ask to be removed at any time by replying to any email.

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts. Any embedded content (such as GitHub Discussions or Google Fonts) may load resources from their own servers, governed by their respective privacy policies.

Questions about this policy can be sent to bookebukar@gmail.com.
