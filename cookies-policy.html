Cookies Policy

This site itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies.

Some embedded features load resources from third-party services and may set their own cookies as part of normal operation. This includes Google Fonts (for typography) and GitHub Discussions via Giscus (for the comments and reactions on project pages).

These third-party services operate under their own privacy and cookie policies, GitHub's policy can be found at github.com, and Google's at google.com.

Because this site doesn't run its own tracking cookies, there's no cookie preference toggle needed here. If you have concerns about a specific embedded feature, feel free to reach out at bookebukar@gmail.com.
